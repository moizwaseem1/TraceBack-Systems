The Intelligence Protocol

In an era of mass surveillance and data brokerage, privacy is no longer a default—it is a discipline. TraceBack Intelligence was founded to democratize Open Source Intelligence (OSINT). We provide the tools necessary for individuals to audit their digital footprint, secure their identity, and understand the vast web of data that exists about them on the surface web.

Architecture & Capabilities

Distributed Scanning

Our engine utilizes Python-based multi-threading to query 50+ social network nodes simultaneously, reducing scan times from minutes to milliseconds.

Zero-Log Policy

We believe in privacy by design. Search queries are processed in RAM (Random Access Memory) and are never written to a permanent database log.

Open Standard

Built on the principles of the OSINT Framework, our methodology ensures that all data retrieved is legally accessible public information.

Tactical Roadmap (2026)

Phase 1: Surface Visibility (Active)

Launch of the Username Enumeration Engine. Coverage of top 20 global social platforms including Instagram, GitHub, and Twitter.

Phase 2: Breach Radar (In Development)

Integration with 'HaveIBeenPwned' API to cross-reference user emails against 15 billion leaked records from major data breaches.

Phase 3: The Entropy Project (Q4 2026)

Release of our Password Strength Auditor. An AI-driven tool that analyzes password complexity against known brute-force dictionaries.

Horizon Research (Classified)

Our R&D division is currently exploring the feasibility of next-generation privacy tools. These projects represent the potential future of TraceBack.

Deepfake Detection
